DUBSEE

YouTube video translation and AI dubbing for iPhone

DubSee helps you listen to supported YouTube videos in your own language. Paste a video link, select a language, and listen to translated speech while the video plays.

Get DubSee on the App Store

Explore DubSee

The official DubSee website includes supported languages, app features, usage guides, and subscription information. Videos need supported captions. Shorts and live streams are not supported.
